Pages 164 – 165 • Core Concept

Why Do Most Cyber Attacks Succeed?

Technical controls protect infrastructure, but Operations & Human Factors focuses on human behavior. Most breaches happen due to three core drivers:

1. Human Error

Accidental mistakes under stress or distraction—such as sending sensitive files to the wrong email, using weak passwords like 123456, or leaving screens unlocked.

2. Social Manipulation

Attackers exploit emotions like fear, urgency, trust, and authority to trick victims into willingly handing over passwords, OTPs, or physical access.

3. Lack of Awareness

Without proper security training, employees cannot spot fake sender domains, suspicious URLs, or voice/SMS scams before it is too late.
